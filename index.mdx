## Your sandbox, from the ground up

An **instance** is your persistent Linux computer. A **template** chooses what runs inside it.

  <Card title="Run commands" icon="terminal-2" href="/agents-api/exec" horizontal>

    Stop and restart your instance while keeping its files.

  <Card title="Reach any port" icon="route" href="/agents-api/urls" horizontal>
