## Your sandbox, from the ground up

An **instance** is your persistent Linux computer. A **template** chooses what runs inside it.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Run commands" icon="terminal-2" href="/agents-api/exec" horizontal>
    Execute code, install tools, and start processes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep your workspace" icon="database" href="/agents-api/instance-lifecycle" horizontal>
    Stop and restart your instance while keeping its files.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reach any port" icon="world" href="/agents-api/urls" horizontal>
    Connect to services over HTTPS or open a signed preview URL.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring your own image" icon="package" href="/agents-api/custom-image" horizontal>
    Build from a Dockerfile or import an image from your registry.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Back up and restore" icon="copy" href="/agents-api/instance-backups" horizontal>
