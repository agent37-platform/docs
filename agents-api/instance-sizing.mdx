| cpu | memory | disk | Price at the default disk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 4 GB | 2-12 GB | \$4.76 per month (the default) |
| 4 | 8 GB | 2-20 GB | \$9.34 per month |
| 8 | 16 GB | 2-40 GB | \$18.68 per month |
| 16 | 32 GB | 2-80 GB | \$37.36 per month |

Disk bills at \$0.09 per GB per month, included in the prices above at the default disk. Those are the prices of the `default` [instance type](/agents-api/billing#default-and-performance-instances). The monthly price is the rate; the wallet is metered per minute, and only for what the instance holds: running time bills the full rate, and stopped or sleeping time bills the disk alone. Deleting an instance settles its final minutes and billing ends. See [Billing](/agents-api/billing).

A [VM instance](/agents-api/vm-instances) takes the same four shapes, and its compute is 2.5x the default rate.

With `resources` omitted, `agent37-ubuntu` uses 2 vCPU / 4 GB RAM / 12 GB disk and
`agent37-android` uses 4 vCPU / 8 GB RAM / 12 GB disk. These two templates require
`"type": "vm"` and default to 12 GB disk even if you select a different CPU/RAM shape.
Other templates default to 4, 6, 12, or 24 GB disk by shape. Pass `resources.disk` to choose
another whole number within the shape's range.

[`burstable` instances](/agents-api/billing#burstable-instances) start at \$1/month for low average CPU and RAM usage and require workspace enablement.
