# Homepage artwork

Original artwork for the Agent37 Cloud documentation homepage, generated with the built-in imagegen tool on October 9, 2026. The WebP files are compressed versions of the generated originals; composition and colors are unchanged.

The SVG use-case illustrations are authored vectors. They illustrate workflows, not screenshots of a product interface. Card text carries the same information without relying on the images.

## sandbox.webp

Create one polished editorial illustration for an Agent37 Cloud developer documentation navigation card. Landscape 3:2 composition, no text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no border, no watermark. Art direction: a beautifully composed miniature architectural still life, tactile matte clay and lightly grained paper materials, precise isometric geometry, soft studio daylight from upper left, sculptural shapes and long soft shadows. Sophisticated contemporary technical publishing, physical model photography blended with clean 3D illustration. Restrained palette: warm ivory background #f4f1eb, charcoal, burnished terracotta/coral #d76b4f, desaturated sage and pale stone. Make the objects substantial and richly detailed, filling 75 percent of the frame with breathing room. Avoid generic glossy AI art, purple gradients, robot faces, floating code, circuit boards and neon. All three cards belong to one coherent series. Subject: a small self-contained square architectural sandbox on an ivory plinth, an open-top charcoal enclosure with a bright terracotta cube at its heart, fine sand-like ground, a miniature stepped walkway entering on one side, three simple upright cream blocks like a miniature city, and a thin pale sage tile beneath. The visual metaphor is a persistent environment you can build inside. Hero cube has subtle beveled edges, shadow falls toward lower right. Camera looking down at 35 degrees. Background uninterrupted warm ivory.
